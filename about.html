About the Platform

TechForge Academy is being built as a free learning platform focused on practical technology maintenance and repair education.

What learners can study

The current learning paths cover Android devices, iPhone, laptops, diagnostics and electronics fundamentals. Lessons combine detailed written explanations, visual steps, one focused video per module, quizzes and free downloadable PDFs.

Our learning approach

The platform emphasizes progressive learning, safety, exact-model verification and evidence-based diagnosis instead of blind part replacement.

Before you start

Open Canva in a separate tab and complete the actions while you read. This course is designed for active practice, not passive scrolling. Keep one folder named Canva Course Practice and save every module project there.

STEP 1 OF 3

Build the Skill — Home and Projects navigation

  1. Open Canva and create a fresh practice file instead of editing an old finished design.
  2. Follow the lesson focus deliberately. Change one variable at a time so you can see what each tool or design decision actually does.
  3. Save a clean checkpoint before experimenting further. This makes it easy to compare versions and recover from mistakes.
Confidence check: Do not move on until you can repeat this step without following every sentence word for word.
STEP 2 OF 3

Practice With a Real Design — Editor toolbar, side panel and canvas

  1. Recreate the demonstrated idea using your own text, colors and assets rather than copying it mechanically.
  2. Check alignment, spacing, readability and consistency at 100% zoom and again from a distance.
  3. Make one intentional improvement that was not shown in the example and explain to yourself why it improves the design.
Confidence check: Do not move on until you can repeat this step without following every sentence word for word.
STEP 3 OF 3

Quality Check & Export — Pages, layers, uploads and file organization

  1. Review the design for hierarchy, spelling, contrast, margins and accidental misalignment.
  2. Preview the final size and verify that important content is not too close to edges or hidden behind other elements.
  3. Export a practice copy using the file format that matches the intended use, then reopen it and inspect the result.
Confidence check: Do not move on until you can repeat this step without following every sentence word for word.

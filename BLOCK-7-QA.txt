Editorial rules carried forward:
- Each full module must contain at least 2,000 words.
- One video per module is enough.
- Step-by-step images belong beside the relevant steps.
- Free downloadable lesson PDF remains part of each module.
- Free-course positioning remains prominent.
- Certificate remains a completion certificate, not a regulated professional credential.
- Future monetization must be implemented conservatively and reviewed against current Google AdSense policies before production.

IMPORTANT:
This block is an incremental migration. It does not invent Android-specific technical procedures where the existing generic smartphone lesson would require model-specific verification. Those technical refinements should be handled carefully in subsequent editorial blocks.

TECHFORGE ACADEMY — YOUTUBE VIDEO IDS

HOW TO USE
Replace YOUTUBE_VIDEO_ID in each lesson HTML with the 11-character YouTube video ID.
Example:
YouTube URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ
Video ID: dQw4w9WgXcQ

You can also paste a full YouTube URL into data-youtube-video; the script accepts standard YouTube, youtu.be, Shorts and Live URLs.

IMPORTANT
- Use only videos the client owns, has permission to embed, or that are legitimately embeddable.
- One focused video is enough for each module/lesson unit.
- Written course content, visual steps and the free PDF remain available independently of the video.
- The embed uses youtube-nocookie.com and lazy loading.
